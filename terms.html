Terms of Use

Lada lets you collect photos for one trip and render them as an MP4 film on this device.

Film Credits

Rendering a film spends 1 Film Credit after the file is stored in the app. Previews are free. If you cancel, or if the file cannot be saved, no credit is spent. Credits are consumable, stay on this device, and are not restored after the app is removed or its data is cleared.

Packs

Pack names and prices are shown before you confirm an App Store purchase. A verified purchase adds Film Credits once for that transaction. A failed, canceled, or unverified purchase does not add credits.

Your content

Photos and films you add remain yours. Deleting a trip removes that trip from the app. A film that was already rendered keeps its own frames.

Locks

An optional 4-digit lock hides a trip inside the app. There is no recovery if you forget it.
