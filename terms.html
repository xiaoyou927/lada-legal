Terms of Use

These terms cover the Lada app. Lada lets you keep photos for one trip and render them as an MP4 film on this device. By using Lada you agree to these terms. If you do not agree, do not use the app.

The app

You can start a trip without an account. A trip has a name, a destination, dates, a cover, and photos. You can type a caption or use Speak Note and then confirm the text. A preview plays the photos in the app and does not spend a Film Credit. Rendering writes an MP4 after you confirm. You can then play that film and, separately, save it to Photos.

Lada works on this device. Trips and films are not posted to a public feed, and there is no cloud library.

Your content

Photos, captions, and films you add remain yours. You are responsible for having the rights to the photos you import. Do not use Lada to store or publish material that infringes someone else's rights or that you are not allowed to keep.

Deleting a trip removes that trip from the app. A film that was already rendered keeps its own frames unless you delete the film. Deleting a film inside Lada does not remove a copy you already saved to Photos.

Film Credits

Rendering a film spends 1 Film Credit after the MP4 is stored in the app. Previews are free. If you cancel, or if the file cannot be saved, no credit is spent. Saving a film to Photos does not spend another credit.

Film Credits are consumable and stay on this device. Lada does not provide a restore purchase control. Credits are not returned if you remove the app, clear its data, or return to the welcome screen with Log Out. Log Out only leaves the home screen. It does not delete trips or credits. Clear All Data does delete them, and that deletion cannot be undone.

Packs

Pack names and prices are shown before you confirm an App Store purchase. Apple processes the payment. A verified purchase adds Film Credits once for that transaction. A failed, canceled, or unverified purchase does not add credits. Prices shown in the app are the prices for those packs.

Locks

You may set an optional 4-digit lock on a trip. The lock hides that trip inside the app until the code is entered. Lada cannot recover a forgotten code. The sample Reykjavik trip uses the code 2580.

Permissions

Camera, photo library, microphone, and speech recognition are requested when you use the matching control. A tracking prompt and a notification prompt may appear once when you first open the trip list. Allowing or denying them does not block trips or films. Lada does not use those prompts to track you or to send notifications.

Acceptable use

Do not misuse the app to break the law, to interfere with the device, or to attempt to alter purchase records. Local Film Credit records follow a successful verified purchase and a successful film render, as described above.
