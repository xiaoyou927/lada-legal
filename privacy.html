Privacy Policy

This policy explains how the Lada app handles information. Lada is a travel film journal. You collect photos for a trip, add short notes, preview a film, and render an MP4 on this device. Lada does not require an account.

Information stored on this device

Lada keeps the following in the app's local storage. It is not uploaded to a Lada server.

  • Trip names, destinations, start and end dates, and cover photos
  • Photos you take or choose, their order, and the captions you type or accept from speech
  • Optional 4-digit trip locks, stored only as a hash. Lada does not keep the code in readable form
  • Unfinished trip drafts
  • Finished MP4 films, the style, frame rate, score name, and whether a film was saved to Photos
  • Film Credit balance, a local ledger, and the App Store transaction identifier for a completed pack purchase
  • A flag that remembers whether the system notification prompt has already been shown

What Lada does not collect

  • No account, name, email address, phone number, or password
  • No location, map history, or travel itinerary beyond the destination text you type
  • No public profile, feed, or social graph
  • No advertising identifier, and no tracking permission request
  • No notification content. The system notification prompt may appear once. Lada does not schedule or send notifications

Photos, camera, and films

The camera is used only when you take a trip photo or a cover. The photo library is used only when you choose a photo or when you save a finished film to Photos. Those system prompts appear when you tap the related control, not at launch.

Photos and films stay in the app until you delete the trip, delete the film, or clear all data. Saving a film to Photos creates a copy in your library. Deleting the film inside Lada does not delete that library copy.

Microphone and speech

The microphone and speech recognition are used only when you tap Speak Note. Lada uses the result to fill a caption. You confirm the text before it is saved. Lada does not keep an audio file of the note.

Speech recognition uses Apple's speech service for that request. Lada does not send the recording to a Lada server.

Purchases

Pack purchases are processed by Apple. Lada receives a transaction identifier so the same purchase is not added twice. Film Credits stay on this device. They are consumable. Lada does not offer a restore control, and credits are not returned after you remove the app or clear its data. A canceled, failed, or unverified purchase does not add credits.

How long information stays

Information stays until you delete it. Deleting a trip removes that trip from the app. A film that was already rendered keeps its own frames. Clear All Data removes trips, photos, films, drafts, purchase history stored in the app, and remaining Film Credits from this device. Removing the app also removes this local store.

Children

Lada is a general travel journal. It is not directed at children, and it does not ask for an account.

Changes
