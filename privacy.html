Privacy Policy

This policy describes the Lada iPhone app. Lada keeps trip photos and finished films on your device.

Information stored on this device

  • Trip names, destinations, dates, covers, optional lock codes stored only as a hash, and photo notes
  • Photos and MP4 films you create in the app
  • Film Credit balance, purchase transaction identifiers, and a local ledger
  • A flag that remembers whether the tracking and notification prompts have already been shown

Lada does not require an account and does not upload trips or films to a Lada server.

Permissions

  • Tracking: the system prompt appears once when you first open the trip list. Lada does not read the advertising identifier and does not change features based on your choice.
  • Notifications: the system prompt appears once after the tracking prompt. Lada does not schedule or send notifications.
  • Camera: requested when you take a trip photo or cover.
  • Photo library: requested when you choose a photo or save a film to Photos.
  • Microphone and speech recognition: requested when you tap Speak Note.

Purchases

Pack purchases are processed by Apple. Film Credits stay on this device and are removed if you clear app data.

Contact

Support stays on this device. This page does not collect a message.
